<h3>Floorball NSG League 3 2025</h3>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">

<h3>Young Technopreneur Challenge 2025</h3>
<p>The Young Technopreneur Challenge 2025 Finals held on Saturday, 26 July
2025 is a competition that challenges students to step up as changemakers—designing
bold, impactful solutions aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals,
Singapore Green Plan 2030, and the Sustainable South West Masterplan.</p>
<p>Congratulations to our Infocomm Club members comprising of Faheem Ahamed
(2D), Boddu Chetan Varma (3C), Lee Jung Kye (3F) and Davian Tan Jun Hao
(3F) for achieving 1st place and Best Presentation Award.</p>

<h4>SCDF Award&nbsp;for saving a man's life</h4>
<p>We are proud of Darryl Lee (4D 2021) who raced as a first responder to
someone who just had a heart attack.&nbsp;</p>
<p>As Peer Support Leader Vice-President, Darryl was naturally other-centred
and as Track &amp; Field Vice-Captain, his training gave him speed.</p>
<p>What also served Darryl to serve others was first aid he had picked up
from a Healthcare Elective Module in school. He had received an award for
good performance in that course but helping to save someone tops any award.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Read the full Straits Times article&nbsp;<a href="https://www.straitstimes.com/singapore/community/six-receive-scdf-award-for-saving-a-mans-life-fighting-fire" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>
