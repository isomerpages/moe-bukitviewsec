<h4>OBJECTIVES</h4>
<p>We seek to bring out the very best in every boy that comes under our charge.
Our boys are given ample opportunities to hone their leadership and organisation
skills through the weekly parades and engaging activities such as camps,
outdoor sports, and adventure races. The boys also participate actively
in the community through BB Cares and BB Share-A-Gift.</p>

<h4>HIGHLIGHTS OF THE YEAR</h4>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Weekly parades consisting of drills, badgework, like-skill lessons, fitness
routines and games</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Activities organised throughout the year include camps (Adventure Camp
and Leadership Camp), friendly sports matches with other, laser tag and
first aid lessons.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Community service activities such as BB Cares, BB Sharity Giftbox and
The Orchard Road Christmas light-up.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>National competitions such as BB Adventure Quest, BB Blaze and BB Character
Quest.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Overseas cultural immersion trip to Ipoh.</p>
</li>
</ul>
